B. Datasets the built-in model was trained on
=============================================

The datasets below are not included in this release. NovoCRF's built-in model (its weights) was trained on data
that include them.

7. PROSPECT PTMs (fragment ion intensities and retention times)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Wilhelmlab, Technical University of Munich, School of Life Sciences. Licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  - PROSPECT PTMs - Fragment Ion Intensity Prediction (MS2):
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wilhelmlab/prospect-ptms-ms2 (doi:10.57967/hf/3379)
  - PROSPECT PTMs - Retention Time Prediction: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wilhelmlab/prospect-ptms-irt
Used to train the model's fragment-intensity predictor (HCD spectra, together with NovoCRF's own training spectra)
and its retention-time (iRT) predictor. The data were converted to NovoCRF's training format; the model's weights
are derived from them. Sources and references:
  - Shouman O, Gabriel W, Giurcoiu V-G, Sternlicht V, Wilhelm M. PROSPECT: Labeled tandem mass spectrometry
    dataset for machine learning in proteomics. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 35 (2022).
  - Gabriel W, Shouman O, Wilhelm M. PROSPECT PTMs datasets (Multi-PTM, TMT, TMT-PTM, Test-PTM), Zenodo
    (doi:10.5281/zenodo.11472525, 10.5281/zenodo.8221499, 10.5281/zenodo.11474099, 10.5281/zenodo.11477731).
  - Based on the ProteomeTools data (Zolg DP et al., Nature Methods 14:259-262, 2017; Zolg DP et al., Molecular &
    Cellular Proteomics 17:1850-1863, 2018).


8. InstaNovo dataset (InstaNovo-FM training corpus)
---------------------------------------------------
InstaDeep. https://huggingface.co/datasets/InstaDeepAI/InstaNovo, https://huggingface.co/datasets/InstaDeepAI/CompOmics_PRIDE, https://huggingface.co/datasets/InstaDeepAI/ms_proteometools
The spectra derive from public submissions to PRIDE (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/); their use is governed by the
EMBL-EBI Terms of Use, https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/terms-of-use/. Spectra and peptide-spectrum matches from this
corpus are part of the model's training data; the model's weights are derived from them.
